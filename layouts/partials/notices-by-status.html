{{/* Returns (dict "current" ... "expired" ...) — the notices section split on `expires` against now. Renders nothing. Every *listing* must go through this; a listing that re-derives "recent notices" on its own will silently drift back into showing expired ones (issues #36, #38). Feeds are the exception: a feed is an archive and items are never removed from one, which is why the notices section feed was dropped (#55) rather than filtered, and why `home.rss.xml` selects dated pages directly and keeps expired notices. See the Feeds note in README.md, which is the authority (#80). */}} {{- $now := now -}} {{- $current := slice -}} {{- $expired := slice -}} {{- with site.GetPage "/notices" -}} {{- range .RegularPages.ByDate.Reverse -}} {{- if and .Params.expires (lt (time .Params.expires) $now) -}} {{- $expired = $expired | append . -}} {{- else -}} {{- $current = $current | append . -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return dict "current" $current "expired" $expired -}}
