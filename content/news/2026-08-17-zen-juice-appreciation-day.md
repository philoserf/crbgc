The Club observed this year's Zen Juice Appreciation Day on **Saturday, August 8**, with a **Glenmorangie 18**, sipped neat with a few drops of water to open it up. The pour came watching the light fade over **Lake Tapps**, the sunset lighting up the face of **Mount Rainier**.

A round earned it, for once — morning dew swept at **High Cedars** with friends of the Club, Josh and Dickie, ahead of an evening spent on the appreciation itself, taken straight and unhurried. The dosage was medicinal, the noticing deliberate.
