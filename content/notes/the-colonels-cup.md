title: "The Colonel's Cup"
description: "The Club's season-long bogey championship, awarded for the best aggregate margin against Colonel Bogey."
date: 2026-07-19
---

The Colonel's Cup is the Club's premier competition, and it could hardly be anything else: the bogey match is the official format, and the margin against [Colonel Bogey](/notes/colonel-bogey-and-the-bogey-competition/) is the Club's only arithmetic. The Cup simply keeps that arithmetic over a whole season.

The Club scores every counting round as a match against the Colonel—who plays bogey on every hole, without effort or joy—and records each Member's result as a margin: so many holes up or down against him. Those margins accumulate across the season, and the Cup goes to the best aggregate. Because the Colonel never has a bad day and never has a good one, the standard is perfectly flat; what the Cup rewards is the Member who, round after round, stayed closest to honest bogey golf. For how the underlying match is played and scored, see the [Match Play Guide](/notes/match-play-guide/).

It is the rare club championship that a steady walker can win over a long hitter, which is exactly the point. The Colonel does not care how far you carry it. He cares whether you made bogey here.
